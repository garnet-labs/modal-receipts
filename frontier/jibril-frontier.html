Works today: destinations + pid/tgid/comm from cgroup hooks on Modal and Vercel (19/19 vs audit, 0–8% overhead); 12 of Jibril's cgroup_skb programs; audit on Modal; fanotify exec/open paths on Vercel.

Needs a Jibril change: non-fatal per-program loading with declared coverage, btf_custom_path, a cgroup connect/sendmsg/sock_create fallback object, proc-connector/procfs process source, and the jb_iter_init_02 split for 6.1 (52/76 → expected 72/76, unverified).

Needs the vendor: BTF + tracefs + tracepoints/kprobes on Modal and Vercel (plus audit netlink and init pid ns on Vercel); MODULES + ftrace in E2B's fc-kernels. Without them, exec/argv/file capture cannot come from the kernel in any of the three guests.
